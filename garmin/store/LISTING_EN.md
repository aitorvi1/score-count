# Connect IQ Store listing — English

## Name

**Score Count**

## Short description

A quick scoreboard for frontenis, fronton and other point-based games, directly on your Garmin watch.

## Description

**Score Count** turns your Garmin watch into a simple scoreboard so you can keep track of a match without relying on your phone.

The main screen shows two large scores, blue and red, plus a RESET button. The interface is designed to stay quick to read and easy to use during play.

### Features

- Add and subtract points for both sides.
- Scores never go below zero.
- Direct RESET to 0–0.
- Score state is preserved when leaving and reopening the app.
- Local history of up to 25 states.
- Haptic feedback when available and enabled.
- No GPS, network access, sensors or external services.
- No additional permissions.

### Controls

Controls adapt to the device type.

**Compatible FULL_PHYSICAL watches**

- Short UP: +1 blue.
- Long UP: −1 blue.
- Short DOWN: +1 red.
- Long DOWN: −1 red.
- Short START/ENTER: UNDO.
- Long START/ENTER: RESET.

**TOUCH watches**

- Tap blue/red: +1.
- Hold blue/red: −1.
- Tap RESET: return to 0–0.
- Physical buttons remain available to Garmin navigation.
- UNDO is not exposed in the touch interface.

Button-event and hotkey behavior can vary with device firmware and watch configuration.

## Privacy

Score Count does not collect or transmit personal data. Scores and history are stored only in the app's local storage on the watch.
